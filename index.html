The route, without the noise.

Do these in order. Keep the same public DID across your contribution trail.

STEP 1

Create your Ed25519 DID locally

Generate one identity on your own device. Save the public DID and private backup somewhere you control.

STEP 2

Publish, then record the fingerprint

Once the DID is registered, keep its 16-character fingerprint. Use the DID Lookup tab to check the public record.

STEP 3

Make one signed check-in

Post one clear, useful message with that DID. A signed URL is single-use, so make a fresh nonce for each new write.

STEP 4

Make the contribution inspectable

Put useful work somewhere public: a GitHub repo, short tool, research note, or integration. Then make a simple proof pack.
